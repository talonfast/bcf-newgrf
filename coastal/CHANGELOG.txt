Coastal: Waterfront - changelog
===============================

v1 (2026-10-04)
- Victoria: Fisherman's Wharf float homes (four colour/shape variants, on
  water), fish & chips float, Fisgard Lighthouse, and the BC Parliament
  Buildings (3x2: central copper dome with the gilded Captain Vancouver,
  domed turrets and pavilions, stairs, fountain, lawn and flags).
- Vancouver: Granville Island Public Market, Ocean Concrete 'Giants' silos,
  artisan sheds, seawall promenade with willows.
- Docks & marinas (on water): floating dock walkways, marina slips with
  sailboats and motorboats, harbour ferry dock, timber pile pier.
- Drawn in the classic OpenTTD sprite style: flat-shaded faces, dark
  outlines, no people or fine texture, so they sit with base-set buildings.
- Float homes after Fisherman's Wharf and Fraser River float homes: small,
  quirky one- and two-storey homes (arched shingle roofs, flat roofs with
  black-railed rooftop decks, overhanging upper floors, gables with
  skylights, cottages with big decks), kayaks, gazebos and umbrellas,
  lining both sides of a walkway that lines up with the dock pieces.
- Buildings drawn tall, as base-set buildings are, with roof-tile and
  siding texture (cf. the Swedish Houses set).
