BC Aviation: Aircraft - changelog
=================================

v3 (2026-10-04)
- New: Harbour Air seaplanes for TGTFTD: DHC-2 Beaver, DHC-3T Turbo Otter,
  DHC-6 Twin Otter and Cessna 208B Grand Caravan EX (from PNW Aviation v4).
  Without TGTFTD they are hidden.
- New: Helijet Sikorsky S-76 helicopter with its own rotor, in any build.
- The airliners are unchanged.

v2 (2026-10-04)
- Alaska Airlines in its new livery: aurora tail (navy, royal blue, teal),
  aft-fuselage swoosh widening from the wing to the tail, teal nacelle
  sweeps, large "Alaska" script; the tail face is retired. Applies to the
  737-900ER, 737 MAX 9 and Horizon E175.
- New: Alaska Airlines Boeing 787-9.

v1 (2026-10-04)
- Air Canada A220-300, A321, 737 MAX 8, 787-9, 777-300ER and Air Canada
  Express Dash 8-400; Alaska 737-900ER, 737 MAX 9 and Horizon E175;
  WestJet 737-800. Current liveries, using the airlines' official vector
  logos (Air Canada wordmark and rondelle, Air Canada Express, Alaska,
  Horizon, WestJet wordmark and leaf). Cockpit windscreens, flat-shaded
  wings, fins in proportion to the fuselage.
