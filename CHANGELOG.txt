BC Ferries, MV Coho & Victoria Clipper - changelog
==================================================

v7 (2026-10-03)
- The rest of the terminal, as objects under "BC Ferries terminals":
  ferry terminal building (2x2, roof-top BC Ferries letters), toll plaza
  (tiles side by side, booths, canopy, lattice light towers), vehicle
  holding lanes (two random car layouts), berth ramp and towers,
  berth wingwall and dolphin (on water), passenger walkway (tiles in a
  row), terminal control tower.

v6 (2026-10-03)
- New object: Tsawwassen Quay Market (2x1 tiles, 4 rotations), in a new
  "BC Ferries terminals" object class. Glass hall with glulam timber
  columns and beam ends, louvred bays, tiled end wall, paved surround.

v5 (2026-10-03)
- Fix: ships broke down far too often. Reliability now decays like
  OpenTTD's own ships (5; 7 for pre-1985 classes, 10 for the PacifiCat,
  20 for the cardboard Pirate Pak) instead of 20 for everything.

v4 (2026-10-03)
- Victoria Clipper IV and V (passenger-only fast catamarans, Union Jack livery).
- Stack smoke spawned at each ship's real exhaust outlets (lighter for LNG/hybrid).
- Salish class: central tower with overhanging wheelhouse, gooseneck exhausts,
  per-ship Coast Salish hull art (Orca, Eagle, Raven, new Salish Heron).
- Structure fixes from photos: Northern Adventure single stack; Bowen,
  Intermediate and T-class central houses over open car decks; ramp gantries
  on Quinsam and Quinitsa; Queen of the North red boot-top.

v3
- Lighting: cast shadows, ambient occlusion, sheen, 2x2 anti-aliasing at 4x.
- Shadow on the water; bow wave and stern wake only while under way.
- Age-based weathering (seams, rust streaks, waterline grime).
- Car-deck portals, bridge wings, liferafts, radomes, davits.
- Sister ships as build-list variants; three distinct Vancouver 2010 wraps.
- Fleet scaled up 1.5x (Spirit class ~2.4 tiles); fixed engine IDs.

v2
- 32bpp sprites at 1x/2x/4x; photo-matched liveries and logos; Pirate Pak.

v1
- First release: 24 ships, 8bpp sprites.
