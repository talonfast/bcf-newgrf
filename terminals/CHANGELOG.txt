BC Ferries: Terminals - changelog
=================================

v2 (2026-10-04)
- Renderer measures each object's true height, so tall free-form parts
  (towers, trees, roofs) can no longer be clipped.

- Official BC Ferries logo on the terminal facade at its true proportions.
