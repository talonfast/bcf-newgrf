BC Aviation: Airports - changelog
=================================

v3 (2026-10-04)
- New: seaplane terminals for TGTFTD (from PNW Aviation v4): Victoria
  Harbour Seaplane Terminal, Vancouver (Coal Harbour) Seaplane Terminal,
  Nanaimo Harbour Flight Centre and a wooden seaplane dock. Without TGTFTD
  they are hidden.
- The objects are unchanged.

v2 (2026-10-04)
- Renderer measures each object's true height, so tall free-form parts
  (towers, trees, roofs) can no longer be clipped.

v1 (2026-10-04)
- YVR control tower, YVR terminal hall, jet bridge, airport parkade,
  apron service equipment; YYJ terminal (glass rotunda) and control
  tower; Harbour Air floatplane dock (on water). Objects to dress
  standard OpenTTD airports.
